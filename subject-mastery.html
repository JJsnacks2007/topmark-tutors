Structured subject program

Subject Mastery

Structured group classes built on TopMark textbooks, with a weekly one-on-one session to catch up, ask questions and fill gaps.

What it is

The structure of a course. The attention of a tutor.

Subject Mastery is for students who want to build a subject properly from the ground up, not just patch this week's homework. Students learn in structured group classes that follow the subject topic by topic, then meet their tutor one-on-one every week to go over anything they did not fully grasp.

It is the combination that makes it work: the group class brings structure, momentum and exam-style practice, while the weekly private session makes sure no student is ever left behind by the pace of the class.

The three pillars

How Subject Mastery is built.

Pillar 1

Group classes + weekly one-on-ones

Core content is taught in structured group classes that keep students moving through the subject at a steady pace. Then, every week, each student gets a private one-on-one session with a tutor to ask the questions they did not ask in class, revisit anything unclear and catch up if they missed a week.

  • Structured weekly classes
  • Private weekly catch-up session
  • No student left behind by class pace
Pillar 2

TopMark textbooks and structure

Classes follow TopMark's own textbooks, written for the SACE subject outlines. Every topic arrives in a planned order with clear explanations, worked examples and practice sets, so students always know where they are in the subject and what is coming next.

  • TopMark textbooks for the subject
  • Topic-by-topic teaching plan
  • Worked examples and practice sets
Pillar 3

Assignments and answering technique

Knowing the content is only half the mark. We explicitly teach how to complete and structure assignments, how to read what a question is really asking, and how to respond to keywords like explain, justify and evaluate so answers earn the marks the student's knowledge deserves.

  • How to structure assignments and reports
  • Keyword and command-word technique
  • Answering for marks, not just correctness

How it works

The weekly rhythm.

Consistency is the point: every week has the same shape, so knowledge builds instead of resetting. See which subjects we run on the Subjects page.

1Attend the structured group class for the week's topic.
2Work through the practice set from the TopMark textbook.
3Bring questions and sticking points to your weekly one-on-one.
4Review feedback and consolidate before the next topic begins.
