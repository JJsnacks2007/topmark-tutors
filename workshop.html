Workshop FAQs

Who is the workshop for?

Anyone working towards medicine or dentistry entry: Year 10 to Year 12, gap year students, and parents. You do not need to have started UCAT preparation, and you do not need to know where you want to apply yet.

Do I need to turn my camera on?

No. Come and listen if that suits you. Questions can go in the chat and we will answer them in the Q&A.

What if I cannot make it on the day?

Register anyway. The free UCAT pack comes to your inbox either way, and we will let you know when the next one runs.

The workshop is about getting into medicine and dentistry, and it stands on its own. We run the UCAT Online Course and we will mention it, because the UCAT is the part of the application most students lose ground on. Nothing on the day costs anything.
