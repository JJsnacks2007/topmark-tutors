Private Tutoring

One-on-one tutoring built entirely around one student: their subjects, their gaps, their pace and their goals.

What it is

A tutor whose full attention is on your child.

Private Tutoring is our classic one-on-one program. Every lesson is a private session between the student and their TopMark tutor, so nothing is pitched at the middle of a classroom. The tutor works out exactly what the student can and cannot do, then teaches to that.

Lessons follow the student's actual school work: the topics being covered in class, upcoming assignments and tests, and the areas where marks are being lost. When something does not make sense the first time, the tutor simply explains it another way, at whatever pace the student needs. There is no waiting for others to catch up and nowhere for confusion to hide.

Who it suits

Built for three kinds of students.

One-on-one tutoring is the most flexible support we offer, and it works at every level.

1Students catching up. Gaps get found and fixed instead of compounding through the year.
2Students chasing top marks. Lessons push beyond class level with harder questions and exam technique.
3Quiet students. Students who never ask questions in class ask them freely in a private lesson.

How it works

What a typical week looks like.

Simple, consistent and focused on progress you can see. Read about our subjects on the Subjects page, or compare programs on the Programs page.

1The tutor checks in on school work, homework and any upcoming assessments.
2The lesson targets the topics that matter most right now.
3The student practises with guided questions until the method is solid.
4The tutor sets focused work for the week and tracks what to review next lesson.
